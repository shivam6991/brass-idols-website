Our workshop

Peetal Nagri, where the moulds are still packed by hand

We work out of Moradabad — the brass city — with a small group of casters, most of whom learned the trade from a parent. A piece starts as a wax or wooden model, gets packed into a sand mould, and takes molten brass at around 1,000°C. It comes out rough. Everything after that is filing, chasing and polishing by hand, which is why two idols of the same god are never quite identical.

We do not plate, we do not fill with resin, and we do not round the weights up. If a listing says 3.4 kg, that is what the scale said. Ask us for photos of the exact piece before you pay, and you will get them the same day.
