## Read this before you sell anything

These are not photographs of your stock. Listing a photo of an idol you do not
actually sell is the fastest way to get a refund demand and a bad review — the
customer opens the crate and it is a different piece. Treat every photo here as
a placeholder with a deadline.

## Shooting your own

You do not need a studio. A phone on a windowsill beats a bad studio shot.

- **Light**: indirect daylight, next to a window, never direct sun. Brass blows
  out to white in hard light and the carving disappears.
- **Background**: a sheet of cream or grey paper curved up behind the piece. The
  stock photos here work because the background is plain and dark — that is what
  makes the metal read as warm.
- **Angle**: shoot at the idol's eye level, not down at it.
- **Three frames per piece**: front, three-quarter, and one close crop on the
  face or the most detailed carving.
- **Square**, about 1000 × 1000 px, under 300 KB. Compress free at squoosh.app.
- Shoot every idol against the *same* background. A grid where each photo has a
  different backdrop looks like a marketplace listing, not a shop.
